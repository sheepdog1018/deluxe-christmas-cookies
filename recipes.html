Deluxe Sugar Cookies

A Christmas favorite, made with love.

Chill 2–3 hours375°F / 190°CBake 8–10 min

Ingredients

Tick off ingredients as you bake.

Instructions

  1. Mix margarine, confectioners’ sugar, egg, vanilla, and almond extract.
  2. Blend in flour, baking soda, and cream of tartar. Add extra flour until nearly stiff.
  3. Chill dough 2–3 hours.
  4. Heat oven to 375°F (190°C). Divide dough in half.
  5. Roll on lightly floured surface to about 1/2-inch thickness and cut shapes.
  6. Bake 8–10 minutes or until edges are just lightly brown.
RECIPE No. 02❋

Vintage Christmas Cookie Icing

The perfect finishing touch.

Mix until creamyAdjust milk to spread

Ingredients

Tick off ingredients as you bake.

Instructions

  1. Start with less milk and add gradually.
  2. Mix on low speed until creamy, not fluffy.
  3. Adjust milk to reach spreading consistency.
  4. Let icing set on cookies for a soft center and slightly firm exterior.

A little decorating magic: Divide the icing into bowls and add festive food coloring for Christmas shapes.
